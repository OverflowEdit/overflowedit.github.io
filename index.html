How it works

You keep control of priorities and approvals. Every video comes back graded, mixed, titled, and captioned, ready to publish.

  1. Day 1

    Kickoff

    A 30-minute call to agree on style, specs, and priorities. Then share a folder with your footage and brand guide.

  2. Within 5 business days

    Rough cut

    Your first rough cut arrives on a private review link. After that, videos follow your package schedule.

  3. 2 rounds included

    Review

    You and your stakeholders leave time-stamped comments right on the video. There's nothing to install.

  4. On schedule

    Delivery

    Final files in the formats you need, with captions and a transcript, ready to publish.

Questions

Do you film, or only edit?

We edit. Your team, a student crew, or a videographer handles the shoot, and we take it from raw footage to finished video.

How do you get our footage?

Share a Box, Google Drive, OneDrive, or Dropbox folder. Footage stays in your storage, and we delete our working copies after delivery on request.

What if a video runs longer than 15 minutes?

It counts as one video for each 15 minutes or part of it. A 40-minute lecture recording uses three videos from your package.

Can you match our brand?

Yes. Send your brand guide, fonts, and logo files, and we'll build titles, lower thirds, and end slates to match. Semester and larger packages include a reusable template.

What does a revision round cover?

One consolidated set of notes from your team, left as time-stamped comments on the review link. Extra rounds are $25 each.

How does billing work with a university?

We provide a W-9, complete your supplier onboarding, and invoice as videos are delivered. Purchase orders and net-30 terms are fine.

Who owns the finished videos?

You do. Once an invoice is paid, the finished videos and captions are yours to publish however you like.
