The work

Do you film, or only edit?

We edit. Your team, a student crew, or a videographer handles the shoot, and we take it from raw footage to finished video.

What if a video runs longer than 15 minutes?

It counts as one video for each 15 minutes or part of it. A 40-minute lecture recording uses three videos from your package.

Can you match our brand?

Yes. Send your brand guide, fonts, and logo files, and we'll build titles, lower thirds, and end slates to match. Semester and larger packages include a reusable template.

What does a revision round cover?

One consolidated set of notes from your team, left as time-stamped comments on the review link. Extra rounds are $25 each.

How are captions made?

We start from an automatic transcript, then a person checks every line against the audio, correcting names, technical terms, and timing. You get SRT and VTT caption files plus a plain-text transcript.

Purchasing and data

How does billing work with a university?

Overflow Edit is a sole proprietorship. We provide a W-9, complete your supplier onboarding, and invoice as videos are delivered. Purchase orders and net-30 terms are fine.

How do you get our footage?

Share a Box, Google Drive, OneDrive, or Dropbox folder. Your footage stays in your storage. Our working copies are deleted once the 6-month archive period ends, or sooner if you ask.

How do you handle footage that shows students?

We follow your institution's FERPA requirements for any footage that shows students, such as lecture recordings or class sessions, and will complete your FERPA training if you ask.

Will you sign an NDA or data agreement?

Yes. We'll sign your institution's NDA or data protection agreement before any footage is shared.

Who owns the finished videos?

You do. Once an invoice is paid, the finished videos and captions are yours to publish however you like.
